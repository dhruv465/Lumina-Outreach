The content from the provided URL describes the TwiML™ Voice: `<Connect>` verb. This verb is used to connect Voice calls (over PSTN or SIP) to other Twilio services or external services.

[1]Key information from the page includes:
*   **Purpose**: `<Connect>` works with nested TwiML nouns to facilitate connections.
*[1]   **Attributes**:
    *   `action`: A URL where Twilio sends an HTTP request when the `<Connect>` verb ends.
 [1]   *   `method`: Specifies whether Twilio uses `GET` or `POST` for the `action` URL (defaults to `POST`).
*[1]   **Nested Nouns**:
    *   `<ConversationRelay>`: Connects a call to a ConversationRelay service.
 [1]   *   `<Room>`: Connects a call to a Programmable Video Room.
 [1]   *   `<Stream>`: Starts a bidirectional MediaStream.
 [1]   *   `<VirtualAgent>`: Connects a call to a Dialogflow VirtualAgent.

[1]The document also notes that `<Connect><Stream>` is available in Ireland (IE1) and Australia (AU1) regions, but regional support is not available for `<Room>` or `<VirtualAgent>`.[1]
